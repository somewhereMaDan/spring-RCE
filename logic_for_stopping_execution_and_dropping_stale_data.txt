The new-run flow -> handleRun stores the new runId in currentRun before the job is queued. The worker stamps that same runId into the header of every record it publishes. So run B's records match currentRun and pass. The old run's records, whether already sitting in Kafka or still being published by worker A, carry the old runId and are dropped.
